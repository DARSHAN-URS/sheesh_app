Welcome to Sheesh (शीश) — India's premier hyperlocal marketplace connecting you directly with the legendary master artisans, brass casters, and traditional designers of Moradabad, the world-renowned "Peetal Nagri" (Brass City).

• Wood & Bone Inlay: Traditional sheesham woodwork, bone inlay decorative boxes, and hand-carved furniture accents.

🌟 KEY APP FEATURES FOR BUYERS:
• Direct Artisan Connection: Know the face, workshop, and heritage story behind every product you buy.

• Dual Profile System: Maintain your personal shopping profile and easily switch to your artisan storefront anytime.
• Transparent Pricing: Artisans receive the full value of their craftsmanship without broker cuts.
• Express Pan-India Delivery: Carefully packaged brass and delicate craft logistics straight to your doorstep.
• Genuine Reviews & Ratings: Verified customer testimonials with real buyer photos.
• Secure Digital Payments: Pay easily via UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

🏪 FOR MORADABAD ARTISANS & SELLERS:
• Instant Storefront Setup: Open your digital shop in under 2 minutes right from the app.
• Independent Brand Identity: Customize your store logo, brand tagline, artisan story, and workshop location independently from your personal buyer profile.
• Easy Product Management: Upload high-resolution craft photos, set real-time stock levels, and customize product variations.
